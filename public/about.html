Our Mission

Paws and Hope helps prospective dog owners explore breeds in a low pressure, visual way. Choosing a dog is a big commitment, our goal is to make the early research phase fun, informative, and personal. Users can browse a variety of breeds, see photos, and save the ones that catch their eye to a personal favorites list for later.

How It Works

The app pulls a list of all dog breeds from the Dog CEO API and grabs a random image for each one. When you save a favorite, the breed and image URL get stored in our Supabase database so you can come back to them on the Favorites page. It's a full-stack app built with express on Node.js and deployed on vercel.
